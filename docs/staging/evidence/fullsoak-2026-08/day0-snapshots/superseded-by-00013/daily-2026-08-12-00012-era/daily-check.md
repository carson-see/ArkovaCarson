## Interpretation

- **A1–A6b** are BL-1 criteria 1 and 2. A FAIL here is evidence-invalidating from the moment it is true, not from the moment it is noticed.
- **A7** is the soak clock. Uptime is sampled 5 times and the **maximum** is taken: Cloud Run may route `/health` to any live instance, so a single low reading is a scale-out, not a restart. A regression of the maximum is a genuine restart of the oldest instance and triggers premortem R2.
- **A9** compares against `EXPECT_SOAK_GATE_DISABLED` in the script's config block, not against a hardcoded `false`. Pre-flip (Day 0) that value is `true`; it must be changed to `false` in the same action that flips the repository variable.
- **A11** hashes only `flag_key|enabled`. A row touched without a behaviour change moves the full-row hash and is reported, not failed.
- **A13/A14** assert the instruments themselves are alive. A soak with dark alarms produces confident evidence about an unobserved system.
